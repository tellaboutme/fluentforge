# Content Pipeline

## Inputs

- curated in-house lessons;
- public-domain or licensed text/audio;
- learner-pasted text;
- user-provided transcript or subtitle file;
- links when a compliant extractor is configured;
- learner-created writing and speech;
- optional external free-resource links.

## Pipeline

1. ingest and preserve source metadata;
2. verify rights and allowed usage mode;
3. extract clean text and timed transcript where available;
4. detect language and quality;
5. segment into coherent units;
6. estimate CEFR-relevant difficulty with uncertainty;
7. identify vocabulary, phrases, grammar, discourse, and pronunciation opportunities;
8. generate tasks with deterministic answer keys where possible;
9. validate task schemas and safety;
10. human-review curated/public content before publishing;
11. version content and retain provenance.

## Difficulty factors

- lexical frequency and sense difficulty;
- sentence and clause complexity;
- discourse structure;
- implicitness and cultural assumptions;
- speech rate and audio quality;
- accent familiarity;
- topic knowledge;
- task complexity.

Text difficulty is not a property of text alone; the task and learner background matter.

## Copyright and provenance

- Store a source URL/title/author/license when known.
- Do not redistribute full copyrighted works without permission.
- Prefer short extracts, learner-provided material, links, public-domain, Creative Commons, or licensed sources.
- Generated adaptations must retain provenance and cannot be presented as official source text.
