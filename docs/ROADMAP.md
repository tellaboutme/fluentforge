# Roadmap

## Milestone 0 — Foundation

- bootstrap current stable web/API dependencies and lockfiles;
- local Docker infrastructure;
- CI: formatting, lint, types, tests;
- migrations and seed framework;
- authentication decision and basic profile;
- contract package;
- accessible design tokens and shell;
- curriculum schema validation.

Exit: clean clone can start locally and pass all quality commands.

## Milestone 1 — Diagnostic and profile

- goal/time onboarding;
- self-assessment;
- deterministic grammar/vocabulary items;
- reading/listening samples;
- writing submission;
- optional speaking upload;
- evidence ingestion;
- per-skill profile and uncertainty;
- first daily plan.

Exit: a new learner receives a defensible initial profile and plan.

## Milestone 2 — Reviews and vocabulary

- lexical/phrase objects;
- review scheduler;
- recognition, recall, listening, and production modes;
- phrase-first card creation;
- error-based reviews;
- due queue and explanations.

Exit: reviews persist across days and update separate memory states.

## Milestone 3 — Reading and listening labs

- curated content library;
- transcript-aware audio player;
- gist/detail/inference tasks;
- vocabulary extraction;
- summaries and reactions;
- difficulty calibration.

Exit: learner can complete meaning-focused input with validated follow-up.

## Milestone 4 — Writing lab

- task templates by level and genre;
- drafting UI;
- deterministic checks plus rubric evaluator;
- selective correction;
- error taxonomy;
- revision and delayed transfer task;
- writing portfolio.

Exit: feedback is evidence-grounded and regression-tested.

## Milestone 5 — Speaking and pronunciation

- recorder and upload pipeline;
- transcription;
- timed monologue and roleplay;
- fluency metrics;
- pronunciation targets;
- delayed correction;
- replay and retry comparison.

Exit: useful feedback without pretending accent equals proficiency.

## Milestone 6 — Adaptive engine v2

- prerequisite graph planning;
- learning-gain estimates;
- fatigue and modality constraints;
- weekly balance;
- plan explanation;
- A/B-safe configuration.

Exit: plan quality beats simple fixed sequencing in offline simulations and user outcomes.

## Milestone 7 — B2–C2 depth

- long-form argument and synthesis;
- meetings/debates/presentations;
- academic and professional tracks;
- pragmatics, register, rhetoric, idiom, metaphor;
- multi-source mediation;
- literature and advanced media;
- advanced benchmark portfolios.

Exit: the platform supports advanced work rather than merely adding harder vocabulary quizzes.

## Milestone 8 — Offline/PWA and ecosystem

- offline due reviews and saved lessons;
- sync conflict handling;
- import extensions;
- teacher/coach review;
- public content packs;
- privacy-preserving analytics.
